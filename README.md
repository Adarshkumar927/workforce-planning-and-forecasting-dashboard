# workforce-planning-and-forecasting-dashboard
💼 Strategic HR Insights for Smarter Workforce Forecasting

The dynamic dashboard gives quick ideas of company workforce forcasting and planning for the organization needs the right number of employees with the right skills at the right time. So, the goal of this dashboard is to help HR predict future workforce needs based on historical data, attrition trends, new hiring needs, and business growth.


Short description :-

A dynamic Workforce Planning and Forecasting Dashboard designed to help organizations ensure the right number of employees at the right time. Built using data-driven insights, it enables HR teams to predict future workforce needs by analyzing historical data, attrition trends, hiring patterns, and business growth. This dashboard empowers decision-makers to optimize staffing levels, improve resource allocation, and strategically plan for future talent requirements, ensuring efficiency and organizational stability.



The key tools technologies used to build in this dashboard :-

Power BI Desktop - one of the data visualization platform use for building an interactive dashboard 

power Query editor - Data transformation, cleaning and preparing data for giving right information 

DAX - Use for performing Calculation and adding some measure and conditional logics 

file format - pbix



Highlights and features of dashboard - 

Our organization faced a critical HR challenge — ensuring the right number of employees at the right time to meet business demands. To address this, we developed a Workforce Planning and Forecasting Dashboard that empowers HR teams with data-driven insights. A line chart illustrates employee trends over the years, while a department-wise column chart highlights salary distribution across teams. KPI cards track the average tenure and attrition rate, offering a clear view of workforce stability. Together, these visuals enable strategic planning, better forecasting, and smarter decision-making to maintain a balanced, efficient, and future-ready workforce.
